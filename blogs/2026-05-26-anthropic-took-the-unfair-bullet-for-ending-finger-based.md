Codex is winning the Claude backlash for now, but I do not think the most interesting story is "Anthropic made a pricing mistake." They probably kind of did. The rollout is confusing, the optics are bad, and developers are understandably angry. But the deeper story is that Anthropic made visible a pricing rule most AI software has been quietly relying on: If your fingers are on the keyboard, you get the subsidy.
