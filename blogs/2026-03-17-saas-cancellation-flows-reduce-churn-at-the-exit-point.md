SaaS cancellation flows — the sequence of screens, prompts, and offers a customer encounters when they try to cancel or downgrade — directly determine how much voluntary churn a company loses each month. According to ProfitWell, companies with structured cancellation flows retain 15–25% of customers who initiate cancellation, compared to near-zero retention when cancellations are processed immediately with no intervention [1].
